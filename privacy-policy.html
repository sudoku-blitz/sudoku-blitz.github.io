Privacy Policy – Sudoku Flash

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Sudoku Flash ("the app") is developed by Liam Zafalon, Switzerland (postal address in the Legal notice) ("we"). Contact: sudokuflash.support@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect ourselves

Your coins, records and settings are stored on your device. On iPhone, if you are signed in to Game Center, a backup of this progress is saved in your own iCloud account through Apple's Game Center (we cannot see it); you can restore it on a new iPhone. There are no accounts with email or password. For the daily puzzle leaderboard we store the username you choose and your daily results (section 5). To improve the game, we use GameAnalytics (section 4).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with ads. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy settings" in the app menu. On iOS, the app asks for permission to track via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will see non-personalised ads.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

If you buy "Remove ads", the purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check whether you own the purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt. We do not receive your payment details.

4. Game statistics (GameAnalytics)

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark) to understand how the game is played, for example how long rounds last, which difficulty is chosen, how far players get, how coins are earned and spent, and when ads are watched. GameAnalytics receives a random ID created by the app, your device model, operating system, app version, language, approximate country (derived from your IP address) and these game events. The data is not used for advertising and is not linked to your name. You can switch this off at any time in the app under Settings → "Share usage statistics".

More information: GameAnalytics privacy policy.

5. Username and daily puzzle leaderboard (Supabase)

To take part in the leaderboard you choose a username. Your phone signs in anonymously (a random ID, no email, no phone number). We store your username, that ID and, for each daily puzzle you finish, your time, number of hints and number of errors. Your username and results are visible to all players. If you play the main game, your username and your XP (experience points) also appear on the XP leaderboard. The data is stored with Supabase (Supabase Inc., servers in the EU). Players can report offensive usernames; reported names may be hidden or removed. You can also block a player in the leaderboard; blocked players are hidden only on your own phone (the list is stored on your device and can be changed in Settings → Profile → Blocked players). Please don't use your real full name as username. You can delete your username and all results at any time in the app under Settings → Profile → Delete account, or by writing to the contact address below.

More information: Supabase privacy policy.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the GDPR, you can request information about, correction of or deletion of your data. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, please also refer to the privacy policies of Google, RevenueCat, GameAnalytics and Supabase. Contact: sudokuflash.support@gmail.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address.
