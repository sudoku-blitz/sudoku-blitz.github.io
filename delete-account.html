Sudoku Flash – Delete your data

You can delete your Sudoku Flash data at any time. There are two ways:

Option 1: In the app (immediately)

  1. Open Sudoku Flash.
  2. Tap ⚙️ Settings (top right of the menu).
  3. Tap 🗑️ Delete account and confirm.

Your data on our server is deleted right away.

Option 2: By email

Write to sudokuflash.support@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and your username in the game. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm it by email.

What is deleted

  • Your username
  • Your Daily Sudoku results and leaderboard entries
  • Your XP and rank on the Rank Leaderboard
  • Reports you made about other players' usernames

What is not stored by us or is kept

  • Coins, designs, highscores and settings are stored only on your phone. They are removed when you delete the app.
  • On iPhone, a backup can be in your own iCloud (Game Center). You can remove it in your iPhone settings under iCloud.
  • Purchase records (for example “Remove ads”) are kept by Google Play, Apple and our purchase provider RevenueCat, so that you can restore your purchases and as required by law. They are not linked to your username.
  • Advertising and usage statistics (Google AdMob, GameAnalytics) are linked to a random device ID, not to your name. See our Privacy Policy for how to reset or switch these off.
